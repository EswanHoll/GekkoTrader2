# GT2 Archive — Vision

GT2 (https://gt2.gekkotrader.com, alias gt4old) is the **frozen dual-lane reference desk** for GekkoTrader. It is not a living GT3–GT6 product desk.

## Purpose
- Be the visual and behavioural source of truth that GT3–GT6 chrome is measured against.
- Stay auth-gated: unauthenticated visitors see login only.
- Cost as little upkeep as possible.

## Non-goals
- No new features, no new trading logic.

## Operating rules
- Deploy only to Cloudflare Pages project `gekkotrader2-archive`. Never `gekkotrader` (GT1/live).
- Control: https://api.gekkotrader.com.

## Exit
Retire GT2 once GT3–GT6 parity is proven and Eswan approves.
